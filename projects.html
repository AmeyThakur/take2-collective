01
Dance & wellbeing

Bharatanatyam for Mental Health

An evidence-informed experiential workshop connecting Bharatanatyam and psychology.

About this project

Young dancers explore strengths developed through movement, rhythm, expression and performance, and how to carry them into everyday life. Participants experience, reflect, discuss and discover, moving from lived experience to psychological science and everyday application.

This is a preventive mental health and wellbeing programme. It is not dance therapy or a replacement for mental healthcare.

02
Exam journeys

Take2 × Aspirants

Mental health and wellbeing for competitive exam journeys.

About this project

Preparation can affect much more than performance. Through workshops, group spaces and conversations, this project supports sustainable routines, efficiency and wellbeing while making room for burnout, anxiety, lowered self-esteem and the emotional toll of long-term preparation.

It also explores identity, relationships, comparison, uncertainty and adult life alongside preparation.

03
Everyday habits

Take2 × Brain Health

What can we do earlier in life to support cognitive and psychological wellbeing?

About this project

Focused on adults aged 45–60, this project brings a psychologist and neurophysiotherapist together in workshops and group spaces to explore stress, movement, cognitive wellbeing and everyday practices that support brain health.

04
Education & self-worth

Bhasha & Beyond

Language, academic achievement and self-worth.

About this project

An awareness and psychoeducation programme for 14–18-year-olds. It explores how language and academic performance can shape confidence, especially when a student’s mother tongue differs from the language of instruction. Language is a medium for learning, not a measure of ability or intelligence.
